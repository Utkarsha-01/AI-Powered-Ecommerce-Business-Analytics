# AI-Powered E-Commerce Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce business data to uncover actionable insights into sales performance, product profitability, marketing effectiveness, customer behavior, and customer churn.

The project combines Python-based data analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI, DAX
- **Environment:** Google Colab, Jupyter Notebook
- **Data Analysis:** Data cleaning, data validation, exploratory data analysis (EDA), KPI analysis, and visualization

## Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue trends and business performance.
- Identify high-performing products by revenue and profit.
- Compare revenue across marketing channels.
- Understand customer status, segments, and loyalty tiers.
- Measure customer churn and identify retention priorities.

## Power BI Dashboard

The report contains two interactive pages.

### Page 1: E-Commerce Business Performance & Revenue Analytics

This page covers:
- Delivered revenue
- Customer status distribution
- Product profitability
- Monthly revenue trends
- Revenue by marketing channel

### Page 2: Customer Retention & Churn Insights Dashboard

This page covers:
- Overall churn rate
- At-risk, active, and churned customer counts
- Customer status distribution
- Churn rate by customer segment
- Churn rate by loyalty tier
- Gender and age-group slicers
- Business insights and recommended retention actions

## Key Business Findings

- **Customer churn:** Approximately 53.4% of customers are classified as churned in the overall dataset.
- **Marketing performance:** Organic Search generated the highest revenue among the marketing channels in the analyzed data.
- **Customer segments:** Regular customers contributed the highest total customer spending in the segment analysis.
- **Product profitability:** Product revenue and profit rankings differ, highlighting the importance of evaluating profitability alongside sales.
- **Retention opportunities:** At-risk customers and segment-level churn patterns can help prioritize further investigation and targeted retention campaigns.

*Note: Dashboard values may change when slicers or filters are applied.*
